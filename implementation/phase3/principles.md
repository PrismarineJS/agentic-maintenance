# Review principles from the phase3 survey

The discovery group contains 400 PRs: 198 merged, 151 closed without merge, and 51 open at collection. Four analysts read the selected discussion, contributor replies, original inline context where available, and relevant changed code. This was a survey of review reasoning, not 400 exhaustive executable code audits. The other 100 cases were withheld from initial skill synthesis; the initial evaluation later informed one explicitly recorded procedural revision, with confirmation answers withheld until its predictions were frozen. Full identities, outcomes, evidence and per-case limitations are in [review_cases.jsonl](review_cases.jsonl); foundational issues/documents are separate in [principle_sources.jsonl](principle_sources.jsonl).

## Unresolved disagreement is useful evidence

[ProtoDef #176](https://github.com/ProtoDef-io/node-protodef/pull/176) merged after a benchmark request and an approval with downstream-check/rollback conditions. Later, [extremeheat distinguished unknown numeric enum values from invalid symbolic strings](https://github.com/ProtoDef-io/node-protodef/pull/176#issuecomment-5651567589): proxies may need to decode and re-encode raw integers. The skills ask reviewers to check that contract. They do not infer that merge status settled the disagreement or prescribe unconditional stricter validation.

The older lighting discussion by [Karang in flying-squid #432](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/flying-squid/issues/432#issuecomment-670922051) allows either a reusable lighting package or integration with prismarine-chunk because of coupling. It supports reasoning about ownership; it does not require a new repository for every reusable algorithm. This 2020 source is foundational context outside the 500-PR window.

## Provenance and strength of the conclusions

The verified account participation counts across all 500 PRs are rom1504: 298, extremeheat: 308, Karang: zero; counts overlap. Search covered 17 repositories and both commenter and reviewed-by qualifiers. This cannot support a claim to have learned Karang's recent review style. Older sources are explicitly dated and separate.

Ordinary comments have verified account attribution but generally undisclosed manual authorship. Forty-two target-account records explicitly disclose agent generation, and three contain attributed agent material mixed with other text. Fourteen PRs have only explicitly agent-attributed target content. Those are retained as operational controls, not independent human-policy evidence. Release commands, silent approvals, abandoned PRs, and routine changes also remain in the corpus so objections do not become the sole training signal.

The five skills combine repeated cross-case reasoning, narrowly attributed policies with exceptions, and declared operational requirements. Current-head verification, publication authorization, Astra disclosure, and avoiding duplicate comments are workflow requirements from this task, not invented historical consensus. Skill evidence includes counterexamples alongside supporting cases.

The coordinator cross-checked nine consequential cases from the other analysts against original target comments, including the registry propagation, trivial-accessor, mapper-strictness, shared-representation, branch-readability, promise-chain retraction, behavioral-test, version-helper, and configuration-scope examples. The architecture/code-quality author also reviewed all discovery lesson summaries and the common skill. This is a targeted reconciliation check, not a measured inter-rater agreement study.
